Disease Prediction Module: This module helps farmers detect crop diseases by uploading an image of an affected plant leaf. The AI/ML model analyzes the image to identify the possible disease and provides information about the disease, its symptoms, and recommended treatment to help farmers protect their crops.
The Disease Prediction Model uses Machine Learning and Deep Learning to detect plant diseases from uploaded leaf images. It analyzes the image, identifies possible diseases, and provides the predicted disease name to help farmers take timely action and protect their crops.
